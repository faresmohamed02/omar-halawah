What the lessons cover

Both modules, all four skills

IELTS tests listening, reading, writing, and speaking. Academic is commonly used for university admission. General Training is commonly used for work and migration. The lesson follows the student's goal.

01

IELTS Academic

For university admission, postgraduate study, and bodies that ask for the Academic module. In writing, Task 1 is a chart, table, or diagram.

02

IELTS General Training

For work, migration, and visas that ask for General Training. In writing, Task 1 is a letter.

03

Writing

Task 1 and Task 2: the order of ideas, the shape of a paragraph, and the language that raises the band.

04

Speaking

The three parts: short questions, the long turn, then the discussion.

05

Reading

Question types, where the minutes go, and how to take the answer from the passage.

06

Listening

The four sections, with attention to numbers, spelling, and changed answers.
